01 / ABOUT

Curiosity-driven.
Security-minded.

I’m a PhD student in Computer Science and Engineering at Penn State University, working on secure and trustworthy artificial intelligence.

My research explores vulnerabilities in machine learning systems and approaches to making language models safer, more robust, and more reliable. My interests also span natural language processing, privacy, and federated learning.

I have spent two summers as a research intern at Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL). Beyond research, I enjoy public speaking, sharing knowledge, and connecting with people through thoughtful conversation.
